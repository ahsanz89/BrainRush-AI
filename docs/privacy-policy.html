Summary

BrainRush AI works without collecting your personal data. There are no accounts, no advertising, no analytics, no crash-reporting services and no tracking in this version. The app is blocked from using the internet, so your information cannot leave your phone through the app.

Information stored on your device

To work, the app keeps the following in its private storage on your phone: an optional nickname, the topics you chose, your settings (theme, difficulty, daily goal, haptics, reminder time), and your scores, XP, level, streaks, achievements and activity history. We never receive this information and cannot see it.

Information we collect

None. We do not collect, receive, sell, rent or share personal information, device identifiers, advertising IDs, location, contacts, photos, files or usage data.

Permissions the app uses

Vibration: to give touch feedback if you leave haptics on. Notifications and boot completed: only if you turn on the daily reminder, so the reminder can be scheduled on your device again after a restart. The reminder is created locally and needs no internet or server. The app does not request access to your location, camera, microphone, contacts, storage or network.

Third parties

This version contains no third-party advertising, analytics or tracking software, and we share nothing with third parties. Google Play, which distributes the app, has its own privacy policy that applies to your use of the Play Store.

How long we keep data and how to delete it

Your data stays on your phone until you delete it. Go to Profile > Settings > Delete all my data to erase everything the app stored and cancel any reminder. Uninstalling the app also removes it. Because we hold no copy, there is nothing further to request from us.

Security

Your data lives in the app's private storage area, which other apps cannot normally read. Because the app does not transmit data, there is nothing to intercept in transit. No method of storage is perfectly secure, so keep your phone protected with a screen lock.

Children

BrainRush AI is intended for people aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Your rights

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete on our side. You are in full control of the data on your device through Settings. If your local law gives you privacy rights, you can still contact us at REPLACE_WITH_SUPPORT_EMAIL with any question.

International users
