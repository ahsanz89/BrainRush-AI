## Common questions

Why did my streak reset? A streak continues when you finish at least one session with a correct answer each calendar day, using your phone's local date.

Why did a session give no XP? Sessions finished implausibly fast, and sessions after the daily XP limit, earn no XP. The Daily Challenge gives full XP once per day.

How do I move my progress to a new phone? Progress is stored on the device only in this version, so it cannot be transferred.

How do I delete my data? Profile > Settings > Delete all my data.
