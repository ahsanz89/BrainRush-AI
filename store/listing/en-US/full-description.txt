Train your brain. Challenge yourself. Grow every day.

BrainRush AI turns two spare minutes into a quick mental workout. Play short rounds of puzzles, memory games, mental maths, vocabulary and general knowledge, then watch your level, streak and achievements grow.

WHAT YOU CAN PLAY
• Logic Puzzles: spot the rule behind number sequences and reason out what must be true
• Memory: watch the tiles light up, then repeat the order
• Mathematics and Speed Challenge: mental arithmetic from easy warm-ups to quick-fire rounds
• Word Challenges: vocabulary and spelling
• Visual Patterns: find the shape that comes next
• General Knowledge: science, geography, history and more
• Attention: find the odd symbol hiding in a grid
• Critical Thinking: spot weak evidence and flawed arguments
• Problem Solving: everyday puzzles worked out step by step

FOR EVERY ROUND
• A new Daily Challenge, a mixed workout of 8 puzzles, the same for everyone each day
• Quick, Timed and Practice modes, plus a Mixed Brain Workout
• Hints when you are stuck (they cost a little XP)
• Clear explanations after every answer, so a miss teaches you something
• Friendly, encouraging feedback and a results summary with suggestions for what to try next

PROGRESS YOU CAN SEE
• XP, levels and daily streaks
• 15 achievements to unlock
• Weekly activity chart and accuracy for each category
• Daily goal you set yourself

BUILT FOR YOU
• Modern dark and light themes
• Works fully offline
• No account, no sign-up, no subscription
• Private by design: your progress stays on your phone and the app collects no personal data
• Accessible design with large touch targets and screen-reader labels
• Optional daily reminder, created on your device
